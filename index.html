FAQ

Common questions.

Is there really a free trial?
Yes — every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You can run real prospect discovery, email generation, and full campaigns. Cancel from settings in one click and you're never charged.
How does the AI write personalized emails?
It analyses each prospect's real data — role, company, tech stack, recent funding, hiring activity — and writes one email for one person. No mail-merge, no templates. The voice matches a senior salesperson: short, specific, signal in the first line.
Will my emails land in spam?
Outpulse includes built-in email warm-up that ramps your sending volume across 14 days. Combined with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC verification, your emails are tuned for inbox placement from day one.
Where does the prospect data come from?
An enterprise-grade verified database of 275M+ contacts. GDPR-compliant by default. Verified emails, job titles, company info, tech stacks, and buying intent signals — all refreshed on a rolling schedule.
Can I connect my existing email?
Yes — Outpulse integrates with Gmail and Outlook. Emails are sent from your real address, which builds trust and lifts deliverability.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel from your account settings any time, no email-the-team song and dance.

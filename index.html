FAQ

Things people ask.

Is the trial actually free?
Yes. Seven days, no card, full product. Real prospect discovery, real email generation, real campaigns out the door. Cancel in one click and you're never charged. We're not playing pricing-page games.
How is this different from Lemlist + Apollo + a VA?
Lemlist sends what you write. Apollo finds who you ask for. A VA stitches the two together. Outpulse does the whole loop — finding, writing, sending, following up — from one sentence. One tool, one subscription, no babysitting.
How does the AI actually write the emails?
It looks at the prospect's real data — role, company, recent funding, who they hired, what stack they run. It writes one email for one person. No "{{first_name}} I noticed your company..." templates. The voice matches a senior salesperson: short, specific, signal in line one.
Will my emails land in spam?
Built-in warm-up ramps your sending volume across 14 days. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC verification on by default. Plus the emails are short, personal, and never look like mail-merge — which is the actual cause of spam folder placement, not the technical setup.
Where do the prospects come from?
A 275M-contact verified database. GDPR-compliant. Verified emails, job titles, company info, tech stack, hiring signals, funding events — all refreshed on a rolling basis. You describe the buyer; we pull the list.
Can I connect my existing email?
Yes — Gmail or Outlook. Emails ship from your real address, not a separate sender. Better trust, better deliverability.
Can I cancel?
From settings. One click. No contract, no email-the-team to escape. You can come back later — we don't burn the bridges.
